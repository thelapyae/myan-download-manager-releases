# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Myan - Download Manager and its Chrome, Arc, and Safari extensions do not
collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

## Download URLs

When you start a download or use **Download with Myan**, the browser extension
passes that download URL to the Myan app installed locally on your Mac. This is
necessary to perform the download you requested.

Download URLs are not sent to the developer, an analytics provider, an
advertising provider, or any other third party by Myan. The Myan app connects
directly to the URL's server to download the file.

## Local Preferences

The browser extension stores its automatic-capture preference locally in the
browser. Myan keeps the current download session locally on your Mac.

## Tracking And Advertising

Myan does not include analytics, advertising, cross-site tracking, behavioral
profiling, or data sales.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web
Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
